Key Design Highlights

1. Embedded Verified Logos

Real transparent ECI emblem graphics loaded in circular avatars with proper containment and subtle boundary stroke.

Positioned bottom-right at 7.5% opacity behind KPI numbers with pointer-events: none so card clicks are never blocked.
