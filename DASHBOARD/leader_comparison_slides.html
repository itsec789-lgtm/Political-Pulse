New Capability Brief

Leader Comparison Feature

Group ke top leaders ki side-by-side performance, reach aur 8-sentiment breakdown ko ek single screen par monitor karne ka automated intelligence tool.

📍 Module: Narrative Groups ⚡ Source: Live MongoDB (2.81M Tweets) 🛡️ Impact: Zero Tab Collision
01 · Why We Built This

Kyun Zaroorat Padi? (The Core Problem)

Pehle Narrative Groups me sirf group-level total numbers aate the. Individual leaders ka stance dekhna mushkil tha.

PROBLEM 01
No Side-by-Side View
Agar dekhna ho ki CJP ya Creators Hub me kaun sa leader lead kar raha hai, to har leader ka page alag-alag kholna padta tha.
PROBLEM 02
Blind Sentiment Stance
Ye pata nahi chalta tha ki Anti-Govt ya Pro-BJP narrative me kis leader ka sabse bada haath hai aur kiski reach zyada hai.
THE SOLUTION
1-Click Leader Comparison
Sub-tab click karte hi group ke sabhi key leaders ki trajectory, volume, views aur sentiment breakdown ek screen par aa jaata hai.
02 · What It Does

Feature Kya Karta Hai? (Point to Point)

Screen par aate hi 4 major analytical insights clear ho jaate hain:

KEY CAPABILITY 01
Multi-Line Trajectory Graph
Har leader ka timeline curve ek sath chalta hai. Peak point par us leader ka circular photo badge dikhta hai (bina overlap ke).
KEY CAPABILITY 02
8 Sentiments Filter Dropdown
Anti-Govt, Anti-BJP, Pro-Govt, Positive, Neutral jaise 8 sentiments me se jo select karo, poora graph usi sentiment par switch ho jaata hai.
KEY CAPABILITY 03
Contribution Share (Donut)
Group ke total output me kis leader ka kitna % share hai (e.g. Leader A: 38%, Leader B: 31%) wo donut chart se instantly dikhta hai.
KEY CAPABILITY 04
Detailed Leader Metrics Table
Har leader ke exact Tweets, Likes, Reposts, Views aur Engagement Rate (ER %) ka clear comparative tabular record milta hai.
03 · User Workflow

Ye Kaise Work Karta Hai? (Simple 4 Steps)

Manager ya analyst ke liye workflow extremely simple aur fast hai:

STEP 01
Select Any Group
Narrative Groups me jakar koi bhi group chunein (jaise Cockroach Janta Party, Creators Hub, Media).
STEP 02
Click "Leader Comparison"
Overview ke right side me "Leader Comparison" button dabayein — comparative dashboard instantly load hota hai.
STEP 03
Apply Sentiment & Metric
Dropdown se Sentiment chunein (e.g. Anti-Govt) aur Metric pill dabayein (Tweets / Likes / Reposts / Views).
STEP 04
Dynamic Graph & Export
Graph dynamically recalculate ho jata hai, zero lag ke saath presentation-ready visual report saamne aa jaati hai.
04 · Technical Assurance

Speed, Data & Safety (Manager Guarantees)

Management ke technical aur stability concerns ka 100% solution:

DATA ACCURACY
100% Live MongoDB Data
Koi static ya hardcoded dummy data nahi hai. pulse_master.master_tweets (2.81M records) se direct aggregate hota hai.
PERFORMANCE
⚡ 64.7ms Query Speed
